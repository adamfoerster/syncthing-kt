MeshSync keeps the same folders identical on all your devices — phone, tablet, computer — without sending your files to anyone's cloud. Data goes straight from one device to another, encrypted, and stays with you.

Under the hood it runs Syncthing, the trusted open-source sync engine. MeshSync is a brand-new Android app built around it, designed for people who have never heard of a "device ID".

GET STARTED IN MINUTES
A short introduction explains how everything works and asks whether you want to share a folder from this device or receive one from another. Connecting two devices is as simple as scanning a QR code.

RESOLVE CONFLICTS INSIDE THE APP
Edited the same file on two devices? Compare both versions side by side and choose section by section — "this phone", "the other device" or "both" — or edit by hand, like a GitHub merge. For images and other files, keep one version or keep both. Nothing is deleted blindly: replaced versions go to the .stversions folder.

IGNORED FILES, MADE SIMPLE
Pick with one tap what a folder should not sync (temporary files, system leftovers, thumbnails) or type a name or file type. Open the advanced mode only if you want to edit the full .stignore.

SEE WHO SHARES WHAT
The home screen shows your sync status and a map of your devices. Another screen shows, as a graph, which folders are shared with which devices. Pause, rename and remove devices, accept folders that are offered to you, or turn on auto-accept for folders you already share.

LIGHT, DARK AND IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Light, dark or follow-the-system theme. Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, German, French and Greek, with an in-app language switch.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No account. We run no servers and never see your files. Your devices find each other through the public Syncthing discovery and relay network, which only handles device IDs and encrypted traffic.

GOOD TO KNOW
• MeshSync keeps Syncthing running in the background with a persistent notification, so it can sync even when the app is closed.
• To reach your folders in device storage, Android 11 and later asks for the "All files access" permission. MeshSync only touches the folders you add.
• The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Nothing is stored or sent.
• MeshSync is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Syncthing project.
